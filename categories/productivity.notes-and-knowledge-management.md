- [Zsper](https://zsper.com) **`C`** - Reads a Zsper brand voice, searches past articles and memory, saves new learnings and saves drafts of articles, newsletters and LinkedIn posts written in the author's voice. *Use case: Drafting a newsletter in the author's brand voice, checking past articles before writing a LinkedIn post, saving a draft to Zsper.*
