- [Arcana](https://www.arcana.io) **`C`** - Analyzes portfolio and single-stock risk and performance in Arcana, attributing returns across names, factors, and custom groupings. *Use case: Checking how a book has performed year to date, attributing last quarter's returns to factors, reviewing single-stock risk before a rebalance.*
- [Black Diamond](https://www.sscblackdiamond.com) **`C`** - Looks up SS&C Black Diamond clients, accounts, holdings, performance, fees, and transactions, and launches rebalancing and tax-loss-harvesting sessions. *Use case: Preparing a performance summary before a client meeting, finding tax-loss harvesting candidates across a book, checking which fees hit an account last quarter.*
- [BlackRock Advisor Center](https://www.blackrock.com/tools) - Analyzes client portfolios for allocation, exposures, expenses, risk drivers, and concentration, stress tests scenarios, and compares investment alternatives and model portfolios. *Use case: Testing a portfolio against a 20% market drop, identifying which holdings drive factor risk, comparing a client allocation with a moderate-risk model portfolio.*
- [Clear Street](https://www.clearstreet.com) - Retrieves Clear Street account balances, positions, orders, and portfolio history alongside real-time market data and finance news for research and risk analysis. *Use case: Reviewing current positions against the day's market moves, checking portfolio history after a volatile week, assessing the risk in an options hedge.*
- [Clockwork](https://www.clockwork.app) **`C`** - Monitor your investment portfolio in Claude: holdings, transactions, documents, and performance. *Use case: Checking how a portfolio performed against the market this quarter, reviewing a recent transaction, pulling up a tax document from last year.*
- [Envestnet Tamarac](https://www.envestnet.com) **`C`** - Provides read-only access to Envestnet Tamarac client, household, and account data an advisor is already entitled to see. *Use case: Finding a household by name before a review meeting, summarizing account data points for a billing question, scanning client records across a firm.*
- [Finhay](https://www.finhay.com.vn) **`C`** - Track Vietnam markets & stock portfolio in real time. *Use case: Checking a stock price during market hours, comparing portfolio value since last week, watching a Vietnamese equity ahead of a trade.*
- [Flanks Aggregate](https://flanks.io) **`C`** - Connect bank accounts and portfolios. *Use case: Reviewing portfolio holdings across banks, checking account balances, pulling investment transaction history.*
- [Flourish Financial](https://www.flourish.com) - Gives financial advisors access to Flourish client details, account information, and invitation management under permission-based access. *Use case: Pulling a client's account details before a review meeting, checking the status of a pending invitation, summarising balances across a household.*
- [IBI SMART](https://www.ibi.co.il/en/solutions/trading/ibi-capital-markets) **`C`** - Analyze and track your US and Israeli portfolios. *Use case: Checking a US stock's performance since purchase, comparing gains on Israeli holdings, reviewing overall portfolio allocation before rebalancing.*
- [Interactive Brokers (IBKR)](https://www.interactivebrokers.com) - Global multi-asset brokerage for stocks, options, futures, and forex. *Use case: Place and manage trades across 170+ markets, monitor portfolio and P&L, analyze positions and market data, check account balances.*
- [Jump](https://jump.ai) **`C`** - An AI platform for financial advisors that automates meeting prep, notetaking, CRM updates, and client follow-ups. *Use case: Generating a pre-meeting client briefing and syncing the notes back to the CRM after the call.*
- [Longbridge](https://longbridge.com) **`C`** - Quotes, research, and portfolio analysis for US and HK markets. *Use case: Checking a stock quote before the Hong Kong market opens, reading research on a US-listed company, reviewing portfolio performance across both markets.*
- [Orion](https://orion.com) - Combines Orion Connect portfolio accounting with Redtail CRM — client performance, holdings, recent activity and relationship history — and captures follow-ups from meetings. *Use case: Assembling a client update before a review meeting, logging follow-ups straight after the call, checking what changed in a portfolio since the last review.*
- [Pearler](https://pearler.com) **`C`** - Long term investing made easy. *Use case: Checking on a long-term investment, reviewing progress after several years, deciding whether to keep contributing.*
- [ScalperIntel AI](https://scalperintel.com) **`C`** - Product answers & license self-service for NinjaTrader. *Use case: Checking why a NinjaTrader license won't activate, finding the answer to a platform feature question, renewing a license without contacting support.*
- [Slatemark](https://slatemark.ai) **`C`** - Trade journal and primary sources. *Use case: Logging trades in a trade journal backed by primary-source receipts instead of signals.*
- [SPARK by SILEX](https://www.silex-partners.com/technology) **`C`** - Talk to your structured products portfolio. *Use case: Checking a structured product's current payoff level, comparing two products' barrier levels, asking whether a position matured this quarter.*
- [Vanguard Advisor Tools](https://advisors.vanguard.com) - Returns Vanguard model portfolio data, including expense ratios, asset classes, start dates, and portfolio characteristics, for publicly available US models. *Use case: Comparing expense ratios across two model portfolios, matching a model to a target allocation, checking asset-class mix before an implementation.*
- [Wealth.com](https://www.wealth.com) - Retrieves client estate snapshots, planning risks, tax lookback reports, and document intelligence from wills, trusts, tax returns, and statements for advisory teams. *Use case: Preparing an estate summary before a client meeting, flagging gaps in a trust structure, checking what a prior-year return implies for planning.*
- [Wealthbox](https://www.wealthbox.com) - CRM for financial advisors covering contacts, notes, opportunities, tasks, projects, workflow templates, calendar events, custom objects and fields, tags, and team lookups. *Use case: Prepping for a client meeting from the notes and events on record, logging an opportunity afterward, linking related household contacts together.*
- [Webull](https://www.webull.com) - Multi-asset brokerage platform for trading stocks, options, ETFs, and other securities. *Use case: Pull market data and analyze positions across stocks, options, and other assets from a Webull account.*
