# Connector Snap Stack — The back-room gig night

*2026-10-30 · sixth stack*

**[TBC Events](https://tbc.xyz)** *(new)* · **[GigXchange](https://gigxchange.app)** *(new)* · **[Google Calendar](https://calendar.google.com)** · **[Gmail](https://mail.google.com)** — running in **Claude Desktop**

A UK pub putting on a monthly Friday gig in its back room, one Desktop conversation: check TBC Events for what else is on in town that Friday so the night doesn't clash, search GigXchange for performers free that date, read their press kits and check the GX Index for what a pub gig typically pays, then make a booking offer from the pub's member account where offers are enabled; put the night and the load-in time on Google Calendar and draft the regulars' newsletter in Gmail. Two new connectors that know the local listings and the grassroots circuit, two proven ones that hold the date and the mailing list, with Claude carrying the details between them. The fee conversation and the sound check stay with the people.

*Status: composed, not yet field-tested against a live booking. If you run it, send a Field Report (see CONTRIBUTING).*
