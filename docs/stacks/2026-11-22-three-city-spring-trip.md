# Connector Snap Stack — The three-city spring trip

*2026-11-22 · seventh stack*

**[Stopover](https://flystopover.com)** *(new)* · **[TravelCharika](https://travelcharika.com)** · **[Booking.com](https://www.booking.com)** · **[Google Calendar](https://calendar.google.com)** — running in **Claude**

Two people with a fortnight in April and three cities on the list, one conversation: ask TravelCharika how each city scores in April, from ten years of measured climate, holidays, crowds and fares, and whether May would suit them better (it scores 87 places, so check the three are among them); give Stopover the home airport, the cities and the window so it prices every order of the cities and every departure day and returns the cheapest route, with the flight or train for each leg and a link to book it; look on Booking.com for somewhere to stay on the nights that route gives; put the legs on Google Calendar. One new connector that works out the order and the day to leave, three already on the list that say when to go and hold the beds and the dates; nothing here buys a ticket.

Stopover reached the list through a contributor's pull request (#53).

*Status: composed, not yet field-tested against a real trip. If you run it, send a Field Report (see CONTRIBUTING).*
